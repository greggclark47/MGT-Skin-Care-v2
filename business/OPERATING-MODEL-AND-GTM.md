# MGT Skin Care: external-retailer operating model and GTM

Decision date: September 6, 2026. This plan supersedes the earlier direct-checkout, product-fulfillment, supplier-payout and consumer Premium-enrollment plans for the current phase.

## Business model

MGT is a skincare discovery and referral portal. Consumers explore routines and external retailers, save destinations, and complete purchases on third-party websites. The external vendor of record handles billing, payment processing, shipping, returns, refunds and purchase support. MGT does not collect the retail purchase amount or issue product invoices.

MGT's commercial customer is the contracted third party. Revenue comes from agreed referral or promotional services. Rates, percentage tiers and agreement terms are TBD. There are no active partner contracts, fee schedules, affiliate identifiers or sponsored placements in the current directory. Listing a business is not evidence of affiliation.

Optimize for sustainable contribution and customer trust, not maximum outbound traffic at any cost. Commercial terms must not override ingredient eligibility, create false product claims or silently influence organic recommendations. MGT remains responsible for its own portal operation, representations, privacy practices and applicable obligations; vendor responsibility is not a blanket release of MGT obligations.

## Audience and positioning hypotheses

Primary discovery focus: K-beauty, with South Korea as the primary brand/retailer sourcing market. Initial shopper hypotheses are people in the US seeking a manageable introduction to K-beauty, returning K-beauty buyers organizing their routine, and later country-specific EU audiences. These are hypotheses for validation, not measured customer segments.

Positioning: “Understand your routine. Discover where to shop. Buy directly from the retailer.”

Mid-range to premium quality is a curation objective, not a verified rating. Listing review should record official brand or retailer identity, authorized-distribution evidence where available, product-information completeness, ingredient documentation, geographic availability, seller support, and complaints or recall signals. Price alone is not a quality proxy.

Expand EU coverage by country and language rather than treating the EU as one delivery market. South Korean storefronts, US storefronts and EU storefronts are directory categories, not assurances of cross-border shipping.

## Revenue architecture: negotiate before enabling

| Proposed agreement option | Value delivered | Fee treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot referral | Limited, measurable qualified traffic | Fixed and/or percentage fee TBD |
| Growth referral | Validated conversion reporting and ongoing discovery | Rate, thresholds and percentage tiers TBD |
| Strategic collaboration | Agreed campaigns, integrations or additional services | Separately scoped agreement; fees TBD |

These are negotiation structures, not published packages or commitments. Do not hard-code tier thresholds, minimums or percentages.

Every agreement must specify the legal counterparty, vendor of record, territories, services, permitted branding, fee currency, fee basis, attribution rules, eligible transactions, exclusions, fraud controls, returns/clawbacks, validation window, reporting access, invoice timing, payment terms, disputes, audit rights, termination and customer-support routing. Percentage tiers must specify whether thresholds are marginal or apply to all eligible sales, and whether they reset monthly, quarterly or otherwise.

Illustrative calculation structure, with no assumed rates:
- Approved variable fee = eligible net referred sales × the contracted rate under the agreed tier method.
- Period revenue = approved variable fees + earned fixed service fees − agreed reversals and credits.
- Contribution = collected fee revenue − acquisition spend − attributable content, provider, reporting and support costs.
- Allowable acquisition spend per referred purchase must stay below expected collectible fee contribution. A click or reported checkout is not collected revenue.

Consumer retail payments remain entirely outside MGT. If Stripe is later used to bill contracted businesses for MGT services, that must be a separate B2B invoicing integration, with approved contracts and fee schedules. Do not reactivate retail checkout or supplier transfers to implement partner fees.

## Provisional launch sequence

Timing is relative to an approved kickoff; no launch date or campaign budget has been committed.

| Phase | Focus | Concrete output | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | Positioning and discovery | Interviews, storefront review checklist, merchant-of-record handoff copy | Validate priority audience and vendor criteria |
| Weeks 3–4 | Partner qualification | Shortlist and contract discussion pack, source/rights review, draft reporting specifications | At least one suitable candidate reaches documented commercial review |
| Weeks 5–6 | Private pilot | Working discovery/saved-retailer flow, sample-to-live catalog boundaries, support routing | Core journeys and retailer handoffs pass testing |
| Weeks 7–8 | Contracted measurement | Approved disclosures, authorized links, attribution/consent design, reconciliation dry run | Signed terms and trustworthy reporting before any fees accrue |
| Weeks 9–10 | Controlled acquisition | Small approved channel tests with spend ceilings and comparison groups | Measure qualified visits and validated outcomes without misleading attribution |
| Weeks 11–12 | Scale decision | Contribution review, refund-adjusted quality, partner concentration and support review | Expand only where performance and operations justify it |

Proposed owned-channel work: retailer comparison guides, ingredient education reviewed for claims, onboarding content and clear “how external purchases work” pages. Test these before committing substantial paid budgets. Test paid search or creator collaborations only after economics, rights and disclosures are approved. Avoid sending launch outreach, buying ads or publishing partnership announcements before authorization and verified agreements.

Must-have launch assets: the external-shopping explanation, retailer qualification rubric, independent/paid listing labels, merchant handoff component, partner one-pager, agreement schedule, consumer support routing guide, reviewed educational content, and a measurement dictionary.

## Measurement and operating cadence

Start with hypotheses and a baseline; no conversion benchmark or revenue forecast is assumed.

Track discovery-to-retailer click-through, returning visitors, saved destinations, broken or redirected links, reported seller/support confusion, signed partner coverage, validated conversions where contractually available, reversal rates, collectible fees and contribution per channel. Segment by storefront and actual permitted shopper market where lawful and useful.

Do not claim sales visibility without retailer reporting. Current links are plain external links with no affiliate query parameters or conversion tracking. Saved retailers are portal records, not purchase history. Do not attach skin profiles, health-related preferences or account identifiers to outbound links.

Use a weekly pilot review for customer friction and link health; use the contractually agreed cadence for partner statements, reconciliation and billing. Owners should be assigned for editorial quality, partnership operations, product reliability and finance before launch.

## Controls and launch gates

- Validate the destination, seller identity and permitted promotion before activating paid links.
- Separate the status of a directory listing from commercial-contract status.
- Display paid/commission relationships near the relevant link and in the relevant language. US disclosure planning should be reviewed against the [FTC endorsement guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking).
- Review the actual US, EU-country and South Korean launch arrangements with qualified advisers for applicable advertising, privacy, consumer and tax obligations.
- Obtain permission before using brand assets, product feeds or partner marks; current cards use text names and direct links.
- Publish verified MGT company and portal-support details before public launch.
- Complete browser, accessibility, security, provider and deployment validation before presenting the portal as production-ready.
