How does billing work?

Choose monthly for flexibility or annual for two months of savings. Your plan renews on the same schedule until you change it.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time from your account. Your plan remains available through the end of the paid period.

What happens if I use more than my plan includes?

We will show the available overage pack before it is added, so you can decide how to continue.

When does an upgrade take effect?

Upgrades take effect right away. Any eligible credit is applied before the new plan is charged.
